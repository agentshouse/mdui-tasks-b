`donor-tasks.json` contains the exact five visible task fields generated by
`satnaing/shadcn-admin` at commit `e16c87f213a5ba5e45964e9b67c792105ec74d26`,
`src/features/tasks/data/tasks.ts`. The donor pins `@faker-js/faker` 10.4.0
in its pnpm lockfile and seeds Faker with 12345. The complete generator was
executed before projecting id, title, status, label and priority, so random
calls for the donor's extra fields were retained in their original order.

There are 100 rows, including one repeated display ID. Preserve both documents
and their displayed IDs. Use distinct Room paths (including the fixture index)
and Room/path row identity rather than changing the donor data.

This synthetic fixture is source-only seed input. It is not imported by the
App or included in `dist/`; the running App must read every task from House.
The donor MIT license is preserved at the repository root and in the release.
